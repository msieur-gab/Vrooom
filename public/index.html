    Why families love it

    Private by design

    No account, no cloud, no password to forget or to leak. Your child's car is their identity, and everything they collect stays on your phone. The places you visit never leave it.

    With no server full of families' data, there is nothing to steal. Your family's life stays private, and your child's playtime is nobody's business model.

    A phone that doesn't call

    Vrooom never sends a notification, never counts streaks, never asks you to come back. It opens when you tap the car, and goes back in your pocket.

    The adventure is out there, not on the screen.

    Rewards you can hold

    Every new playground earns a badge you print at home. Kids cut it out, colour it, glue it into their travel passport, and keep playing with it all weekend.

    What gets rewarded is curiosity and going out, not time behind a screen.

    A toy first

    A hand-planed beech car you can take everywhere, and a good toy on its own.

    The app adds the map and the badges. The car never needs it.
